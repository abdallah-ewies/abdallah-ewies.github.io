Income and life expectancy: interactive dashboard (demo project)

World Bank data, 2019 and 2000 to 2019. Filter by region or income group.

Income and life expectancy, 2019

One dot per country. Horizontal axis is GDP per person on a log scale. The line is the fit over the countries shown. Egypt is circled. Hover for values.

Furthest below the trend (shown countries)

Actual life expectancy minus the value the fit predicts for that income, in years.

Furthest above the trend (shown countries)

Same measure, positive side.

Biggest gains in life expectancy, 2000 to 2019 (shown countries)

Years gained. Egypt gained 3.9 years; the median for its income group was 6.7.

Source: World Bank, World Development Indicators (SP.DYN.LE00.IN, NY.GDP.PCAP.PP.KD), 2019 values, 199 countries with both, accessed 24 Nov 2026, Licence CC BY 4.0 per World Bank indicator metadata. Upstream: UN World Population Prospects, International Comparison Program. Own calculations, not endorsed by the World Bank. Demo project. The fit describes an association between income and life expectancy across countries; it does not show that income causes longer lives. The flagged country is one whose value differs a lot from its neighbours' median; the toggle shows the effect of leaving it out. The page fit is recomputed from the dots shown, so it changes with the filters.
