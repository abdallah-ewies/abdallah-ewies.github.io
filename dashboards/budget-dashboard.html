Demo with invented numbers for a fictional coffee-subscription business. The scenarios are assumptions, not forecasts. The model matches the Excel workbook: revenue is earned in the month of sale, part of it is collected the next month, costs are paid in the month incurred, the equipment purchase is paid in cash in its month and is not expensed. No tax, loan, inventory build or seasonality.
