Churn by plan and channel, MRR movement and cohort retention.

The plan filter changes the segment chart and the first three numbers.

Monthly churn by channel

Cancellations divided by customer-months at risk, for the plan selected.

Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)

Bars: new MRR (green) and churned MRR (red). Line: closing MRR. Hover a month.

Cohort retention (share of a signup month still subscribed)

Each row is a signup month, each column the months since signup. Darker is higher retention. Hover a cell.

Synthetic data: 6,040 invented customers generated with a fixed random seed, so every pattern here comes from the generator and is not a real business. Churn rate = cancellations / customer-months at risk. Opening MRR + new MRR - churned MRR = closing MRR was checked for every month. Built from the SQL queries in the project.
