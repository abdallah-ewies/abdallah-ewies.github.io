Subscription churn by cohort

Project 2, demo

OverviewWorld BankChurnBudget

For a made-up subscription product, how fast do customers cancel, which plans and channels churn most, and how does recurring revenue move month to month?

ToolsSQL, Python, interactive dashboard
DataInvented data. A seeded generator creates 6,040 customers (signups Jan 2024 to Dec 2025) in SQLite. Churn depends on plan, channel and customer age through a small table in the script, so the findings describe the generator.

Key numbers

January 2024 cohort still subscribed87% after 1 month, 63% after 6, 29% after 23
Monthly churn by planStarter 9.7%, Pro 5.2%, Team 3.0%
Paid search vs referralStarter 13.4% vs 6.3%, Pro 7.1% vs 3.4%, Team 3.9% vs 1.8%
Closing MRR, Dec 2025About $161.6k
