Income, health and life expectancy

Project 1, demo

OverviewWorld BankChurnBudget

How much of the difference in life expectancy between countries lines up with income, and where does Egypt sit?

ToolsPython, Excel, interactive dashboard
DataWorld Bank World Development Indicators via the public API, 2000 to 2022, 199 countries in the 2019 fit. Indicators: GDP per capita PPP, life expectancy, health spending per person, population, infant mortality.

Key numbers

Main result (all source values)4.1 years more life expectancy per doubling of income, R² 0.715
Egypt, 201971.2 years against 72.6 expected from income (gap -1.4)
Egypt progress 2000 to 2019+3.9 years, against a median of +6.7 for lower-middle-income countries
Egypt health spend per person, 2019$143, against a group median of $71
